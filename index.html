About the Firm

A Law Firm Built on Integrity and Results

NSE Law Chambers is a full-service law firm based in Kigali, Rwanda, dedicated to providing high-quality legal services to individuals, businesses, and institutions. We combine deep knowledge of Rwandan law with a commitment to practical, results-driven counsel.

Our attorneys bring expertise across a broad range of disciplines — from banking and finance to criminal defence and intellectual property — ensuring every client receives focused, specialist attention regardless of the complexity of their matter.

Our Core Practice Areas

We handle a wide range of legal matters across Rwanda and the East African region. Below are some of our principal areas of focus.

01

Banking & Finance Law

Advising financial institutions, lenders, and borrowers on regulatory compliance, loan agreements, and financial disputes.

02

Public Procurement Law

Guidance on procurement regulations, tender processes, and disputes involving public contracts and government entities.

03

Labour Law

Representing employers and employees in workplace disputes, contract negotiations, and employment tribunal proceedings.

04

International Law

Cross-border legal matters including international trade, treaty obligations, and regional EAC regulatory frameworks.

05

Administrative Law

06

Land & Property Law

07

Criminal Defence

Intellectual Property

Meet the Managing Partner

Nshimiyimana S. Eugene is the founding partner of NSE Law Chambers. With over 15 years of legal practice across Rwanda and the East African region, he brings a deep understanding of Rwandan law, regional trade frameworks, and international legal standards.

Eugene has represented clients ranging from individuals and SMEs to multinational corporations and government institutions before the highest courts in Rwanda, earning a reputation for rigorous preparation and principled advocacy.
